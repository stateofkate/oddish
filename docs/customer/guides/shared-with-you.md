# Work Abundant shares with you

Abundant delivers data to your organization in Oddish. Sign in at oddish.app with your work email; people at your company's email domain join your organization on first sign-in. Your view is read-only: you cannot start runs or change anything.

The navigation has three pages:

- **Experiments** lists experiments shared with you, newest first. Open one to see its tasks, trials, logs, files, and Abundant's QA results.
- **Tasks** lists every task you can open: tasks shared on their own, tasks in a delivery, and tasks in a shared experiment. A delivered task shows the delivery name and the version that shipped.
- **Deliveries** lists finalized deliveries. A delivery page shows each task with its shipped version, any note Abundant wrote for you, its checks, and the QA findings fixed before shipping.

## Download a task

Open the task from **Tasks** or from a delivery. Select a file in the tree to read or download it. When the task is stored as a single archive, **Download task** at the top right saves the whole task as one file. The link expires after a short time; reopen the page for a fresh one.

## What you do not see

Costs are hidden unless Abundant chose to show them for a share. Other customers' work and Abundant's internal notes never appear.

## Access

Ask your Abundant contact to add people outside your email domain, or to stop sharing something. A share Abundant removes disappears from your pages immediately.
