# Work Abundant shares with you

Abundant delivers data to your organization in Oddish. Sign in at oddish.app with your work email; people at your company's email domain join your organization on first sign-in. Your view is read-only: you cannot start runs or change anything.

The navigation has two pages:

- **Samples** lists samples shared with you, newest first, with how many tasks and runs each holds. Open one to see its tasks, trials, logs, files, and Abundant's QA results.
- **Tasks** lists every task you can open: tasks shared on their own, tasks Abundant delivered to you, and tasks in a shared sample. A delivered task shows its delivery name and the version that shipped.

## Open and download a task

A task opens on its `instruction.md`. **Overview** shows Abundant's QA for that version, and the file tree holds everything else. **Download task** at the top right saves the whole task as one file. The link expires after a short time; reopen the page for a fresh one.

## What you do not see

Costs are hidden unless Abundant chose to show them for a share. Other customers' work and Abundant's internal notes never appear.

## Access

Ask your Abundant contact to add people outside your email domain, or to stop sharing something. A share Abundant removes disappears from your pages immediately.
