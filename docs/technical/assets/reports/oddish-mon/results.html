Timeline

  • 18 SepProblem agreed with Meji and Kyle: remove the watcher, keep behaviour identical, add a status check before any reap.
  • 19 to 22 SepBuilt and proven on preview environments: parity, isolation, failure drills, a 40-task regression. Rewritten as seven reviewable PRs.
  • 23 SepAll of it on staging at 100%. Production at 1% the same evening, with fallback to the old path.
  • 24 SepImage warm-up: task images build once per task version, not inside every paid trial. Early-death detection within 20 s.
  • 28 Sep5%, after a 24-hour production review found no execution errors or missing results.
